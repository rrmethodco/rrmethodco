## Testimonial Capture (Day 30 and Day 60)

Schedule a 20-minute conversation with mom AND the office manager. Capture in their own words.

**Quotes to specifically ask for:**
1. Time savings — in their words
2. Dollar recovery — in their words
3. The unexpected benefit — usually the stickiest part of the story
4. Referral relationship impact — "what have your referring GPs said about getting letters faster?"
5. Whether mom would recommend it to other endodontists in her AAE network / study club

**Permission to use:**
- [ ] Permission to use practice name in case study
- [ ] OK to anonymize ("a 4-endodontist practice in [state]")
