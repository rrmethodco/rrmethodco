## Testimonial Capture (Day 30 and Day 60)

Schedule a 20-minute conversation with the office manager AND the dentist-owner. Capture in their own words. Ideal testimonial structure:

> "Before, [office manager name] spent X hours every week on insurance paperwork, and we still wrote off about $Y per month in denials we couldn't pursue. After [practice owner first name] brought in this system, we cut that time to Z hours and recovered $W in the first month. The thing I didn't expect was [unexpected positive outcome — usually 'we stopped wasting time on appeals we couldn't win,' or 'I finally have time to actually call patients']."

**Quotes to specifically ask for:**
1. Time savings — in their words
2. Dollar recovery — in their words
3. The unexpected benefit — usually the stickiest part of the story
4. Whether they'd recommend it to other GP practices in their study club / network

**Permission to use:**
- [ ] Permission to use practice name in case study
- [ ] OK to anonymize ("a 4-provider GP practice in [state]")
- [ ] Permission to use dollar figures
- [ ] Willing to take referral calls from prospective clients
